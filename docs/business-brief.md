# CarePoint Clinic: Business Brief

## The business
CarePoint Clinic is a fictional private clinic in Lagos, Nigeria, created as a product concept for this assignment. The app lets patients book appointments, see doctors and prices, and check whether their laboratory results are ready. Doctors, slots, patients and payments in the prototype are sample or simulated data.

Front-desk staff use an admin dashboard to manage bookings, services and payment records.

## The user
Adults aged 18 to 60, mostly on low-cost Android phones (Samsung, Tecno) with slow or unreliable mobile data. Technical confidence varies, and some are unwell or worried about a family member when they use the app. The interface must be simple, lightweight and readable on small screens in bright daylight.

The second user is front-desk staff, who manage bookings and payment records.

## The one thing that must work
**Booking an appointment.** A patient can:
1. View doctors and their specialisations.
2. Check available dates and time slots.
3. Choose a doctor and time.
4. Give the details the clinic needs.
5. Confirm the appointment.
6. Receive a booking confirmation.

The booking then appears on the admin dashboard.

Payment is separate. A patient can confirm without paying, and a payment is recorded afterwards as pending or paid. No payment gateway is used.

## Three questions only this clinic can answer
1. Which doctors are available, and what times can I book?
2. How much does a particular consultation or laboratory test cost here?
3. Is my laboratory result ready?

## Deliberately not built
Doctor management by admins, doctor consultation notes, laboratory processing, viewing or delivering actual results (the app shows only a status such as processing or ready, and the clinic gives the result in person), and online payment gateways.

## The feel
Calm, trustworthy, clear and warm, and responsive on a weak connection.

Healthcare is stressful, so the interface should reduce anxiety and make patients comfortable sharing personal information. A rounded, friendly but professional typeface feels approachable while staying readable. Generous spacing, clear hierarchy and plain language help patients find what they need without feeling overwhelmed.
