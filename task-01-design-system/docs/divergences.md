# Divergences from Material defaults

## 1. Font family

- **Material default:** Roboto.
- **My choice:** DM Sans for all 15 styles.
- **Why:** I chose DM Sans because it has a clean, rounded and friendly appearance while still looking professional. It also gives CarePoint a modern and welcoming feel, making the interface easier to approach.
- **When this choice would be wrong:** DM Sans may not be the best choice for very formal documents or content that requires a more traditional and authoritative appearance. It may also need careful sizing and spacing on smaller screens to maintain readability.

## 2. Heavier heading weights

- **Material default:** Display and Headline are Regular (400).
- **My choice:** Display is Semibold and Headline is Medium.
- **Why:** I chose Semibold for Display and Medium for Headline to make important information stand out and easier to identify, especially for patients scanning the app on small phone screens or in bright daylight. I did not formally test every weight combination; these choices were based on the clear visual hierarchy I wanted for CarePoint.
- **When this choice would be wrong:** Heavier headings may feel too bold on a calm, minimal page or when used with very large text on desktop screens. They may also make a dark-mode interface feel visually crowded.
