# Shadows

Three drop shadows, each built from the primary brand colour `#1D38E2` and not from black. They exist in Figma as effect styles named Soft Shadow, Medium Shadow and Hard Shadow.

| Shadow | x | y | blur | spread | colour | opacity |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Soft | 1.5 | 6 | 30 | 0 | `#1D38E2` | 12% |
| Medium | 4 | 6 | 24 | 0 | `#1D38E2` | 18% |
| Hard | 6 | 10 | 20 | 0 | `#1D38E2` | 20% |

From soft to hard, the blur gets tighter and the offset and opacity increase, so a "harder" shadow reads as an element sitting higher and more clearly separated from the page.

## Why a brand-tinted shadow reads differently

I chose blue-tinted shadows because they create a softer and cleaner appearance than black or grey shadows. They blend naturally with CarePoint's primary blue and give the components a consistent branded appearance while remaining visible against the light grey background.
