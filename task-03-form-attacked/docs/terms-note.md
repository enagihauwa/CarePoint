# Terms note: where the wording will come from

Drafted by Claude at my request from my rough answers, so the wording is not all mine.

## Status: the terms wording is not chosen yet

I do not have a lawyer-approved terms document or a template selected for CarePoint. **No terms or privacy wording has been written by the agent, and none will be.** The guide requires the wording to come from an adapted template or from a lawyer-checked document, not from the agent.

## What I intend to do

- Source a terms-of-service template from a reputable provider and adapt it to CarePoint.
- Have the adapted wording reviewed by a qualified lawyer before the clinic uses it. A reviewer would also want to check how it fits Nigerian data-protection rules for health-related personal information.
- Keep the **privacy notice** and the **terms of service** as two separate documents, because they serve different purposes: the privacy notice says what personal data is collected and why, and the terms say what the patient is agreeing to when they use the service.

## What the form does until then

The registration form has a terms checkbox so that the behaviour can be built and tested (required before the account can be created, reachable by keyboard, announced by a screen reader). Its label is a plain functional line ("I agree to the CarePoint terms and privacy notice") that links to a placeholder page. The placeholder page says the wording is pending. It contains **no legal text**, and it must be replaced before any real patient uses the form.
